\startverse{2}
Vera fides Géniti\\
purgávit crímina mundi,\\
Et tibi virgínitas\\
invioláta manet.
\divverse
True faith in the Son\\
has purged the sins of the world,\\
and your virginity\\
remains inviolate.
\finishverse

\startverse{3}
Te matrem pietátis,\\
opem te clámitat orbis:\\
Subvénias famulis,\\
o benedícta tuis.
\divverse
The world cries to thee for aid,\\
loving mother;\\
O thou blessed one, mayest thou\\
succour thy people.
\finishverse

\startverse{4}
Glória magna Patri,\\
compar sit glória Nato,\\
Spirítui Sancto\\
glória magna Deo.
Amen.
\divverse
Glory be to the Father,\\
and equal glory to the Son,\\
and to the Holy Spirit,\\
to God be great glory.
Amen.
\finishverse
