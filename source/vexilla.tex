
\bigskip

\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{2.} 
Quae vulneráta lánceæ\\
Mucróne diro, críminum\\
Ut nos laváret sórdibus,\\
Manávit unda\lyrlink{}et sánguine.
\end{minipage}%
\hskip10pt
\begin{minipage}{0.50\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{2.} 
Who, wounded with a direful spear,\\
Did, purposely to wash us clear\\
From stain of sin pour out a flood\\
Of precious water mixed with blood.
\end{minipage}
\bigskip


\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{3.} 
Impléta sunt quæ cóncinit\\
David fidéli cármine,\\
Dicéndo natiónibus:\\
Regnávit a ligno Deus.
\end{minipage}%
\hskip10pt
\begin{minipage}{0.50\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{3.} 
That which the prophet-king of old\\
Hath in mysterious verse foretold,\\
Is now accomplished, whilst we see\\
God ruling nations from a Tree.
\end{minipage}
\bigskip


\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{4.} 
Arbor decóra\lyrlink{}et fúlgida,\\
Ornáta Regis púrpura,\\
Elécta digno stípite\\
Tam sancta membra tángere. 
\end{minipage}%
\hskip10pt
\begin{minipage}{0.50\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{4.} 
O lovely and refulgent Tree,\\
Adorned with purpled majesty;\\
Culled from a worthy stock, to bear\\
Those limbs which sanctifiéd were.
\end{minipage}
\bigskip


\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{5.} 
Beáta, cujus bráchiis\\
	Pré\intralink{tium} pepéndit sǽculi:\\
Statéra facta córporis,\\
Tulítque prædam tártari.
\end{minipage}%
\hskip10pt
\begin{minipage}{0.50\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{5.} 
Blest Tree, whose happy branches
bore\\
The wealth that did the world restore;\\
The beam that did the Body weigh\\
Which raised up hell’s expected prey.
\end{minipage}
\bigskip


\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{6.} 
O Crux ave, spes única,\\
Hoc Passiónis témpore* :\\
Piis adáuge grátiam,\\
Reísque dele crímina.
\end{minipage}%
\hskip10pt
\begin{minipage}{0.50\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{6.} 
Hail Cross, of hopes the most
sublime!\\
Now, in this mournful Passion time;\\
Grant to the just increase of grace,\\
And every sinner’s crimes efface.
\end{minipage}
\bigskip


\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{7.} 
Te, fons salútis Trínitas,\\
Colláudet omnis spíritus :\\
Quibus Crucis victóriam\\
Largíris, adde prǽmium.\\
Amen.
\end{minipage}%
\hskip10pt
\begin{minipage}{0.50\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{7.} 
Blest Trinity, salvation’s spring\\
May every soul Thy praises sing;\\
To those Thou grantest conquest by\\
The holy Cross, rewards supply.\\
Amen
\end{minipage}
\bigskip

\emph{* Alternative verse for outside Passiontide:}

\noindent\begin{minipage}[t]{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{6.} 
O Crux ave, spes única,\\
In hac triúmphi glória * :\\
Piis adáuge grátiam,\\
Reísque dele crímina.
\end{minipage}%
\hskip10pt
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.50\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{6.} 
Hail Cross, our Hope, on Thee we call,\\
In this triumphant festival,\\
Grant to the just increase of grace,\\
And every sinner’s crimes efface.
\end{minipage}
