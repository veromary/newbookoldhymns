\startverse{2}
Sumens illud Ave\\
Gabriélis ore,\\
Funda nos in pace,\\
Mutans Hevæ nomen.
\divverse
Taking that sweet Ave\\
Erst by Gabriel spoken,\\
Eva’s name reversing,\\
Be of peace the token.
\finishverse

\startverse{3}
Solve vincla reis,\\
Profer lumen cæcis:\\
Mala nostra pelle,\\
Bona cuncta posce.
\divverse
Break the sinner’s fetters,\\
Light to blind restoring,\\
All our ills dispelling\\
Every boon imploring.
\finishverse

\startverse{4}
Monstra te\lyrlink{}esse matrem:\\
Sumat per te preces,\\
Qui pro nobis natus,\\
Tulit esse tuus.
\divverse
Show thyself a Mother\\
In thy supplication;\\
He will hear who chose thee\\
At His Incarnation.
\finishverse

\startverse{5}
Virgo singuláris,\\
Inter omnes mitis,\\
Nos culpis solútos,\\
Mites fac et castos.
\divverse
Maid all maids excelling,\\
Passing meek and lowly,\\
Win for sinners pardon,\\
Make us chaste and holy.
\finishverse

\startverse{6}
Vitam præsta puram,\\
Iter para tutum :\\
Ut vidéntes Jesum,\\
Semper collætémur.
\divverse
As we onward journey\\
Aid our weak endeavour\\
Till we gaze on Jesus\\
And rejoice forever.
\finishverse

\startverse{7}
Sit laus Deo Patri,\\
Summo Christo decus,\\
Spirítui Sancto,\\
Tribus honor unus.
\divverse
Father, Son and Spirit,\\
Three in One confessing,\\
Give we equal glory\\
Equal praise and blessing.
\finishverse
