\startverse{2}
Qui díceris Paráclitus, \\ Altíssimi donum Dei, \\ Fons vivus, ignis cáritas \\ Et spiritális únctio.
\divverse
Thou who art called the Paraclete,\\
Best gift of God above,\\
The living spring, the living fire,\\
Sweet unction and true love.
\finishverse


\startverse{3}
Tu septifórmis múnere, \\ Dí\intralink{g}itus patérnæ déxteræ, \\ Tu rite promíssum Patris \\ Sermóne ditans gúttura.
\divverse
Thou who art se’enfold in Thy grace,\\
Finger of God’s right hand;\\
His promise, teaching little ones\\
To speak and understand.
\finishverse


\startverse{4}
Accénde lumen sénsibus, \\ Infúnde\lyrlink{}amórem córdibus, \\ Infírma nostri córporis \\ Virtúte firmans pérpeti.
\divverse
O guide our minds with Thy blest light,\\
With love our hearts inflame;\\
And with Thy strength, which ne’er
decays,\\
Confirm our mortal frame.
\finishverse


\startverse{5}
Hostem repéllas lóngius \\ Pacémque dones prótinus; \\ Ductóre sic te prǽvio \\ Vitémus omne nóxium.
\divverse
Far from us drive our deadly foe;\\
True peace unto us bring;\\
And through all perils lead us safe\\
Beneath Thy sacred wing.
\finishverse


\startverse{6}
Per te sciámus da Patrem \\ Noscámus atque Fílium, \\ Teque\lyrlink{}utriúsque Spíritum \\ Credámus ómni témpore.
\divverse
Through Thee may we the Father know,\\
Through Thee th’ eternal Son,\\
And Thee the Spirit of them both,\\
Thrice blessed Three in One.
\finishverse


\startverse{7}
Deo Patri sit glória \\ Et Filio qui\lyrlink{}a mortuis, \\ Surréxit ac Paráclito, \\ In saeculórum saécula.
Amén.
\divverse
All Glory to the Father be,\\
With His co-equal Son:\\
The same to Thee, great Paraclete,\\
While endless ages run.
\finishverse
