\settowidth{\englishwidth}{Seeing, touching, tasting are in Thee dece}
\setlength{\latinwidth}{0.45\textwidth}

\startverse{2}
Visus, tactus, gustus in te
fállitur,\\
Sed audítu solo tuto créditur:\\
Credo quidquid dixit Dei Fílius:\\ 
Nil hoc verbo veritátis vérius.
\divverse
Seeing, touching, tasting are in Thee deceived,\\
How says trusty hearing? That shall be
believed;\\
What God’s Son hath told me, take for truth I
do;\\
Truth Himself speaks truly, or there’s nothing
true.
\finishverse

\medskip

\startverse{3}
In cruce latébat sola Déitas,\\
At hic latet simul et humánitas:\\
Ambo tamen credens atque
cónfitens,\\
Peto quod petívit latro
pǽnitens.
\divverse
On the Cross Thy Godhead made no sign to
men;\\
Here Thy very manhood steals from human
ken;\\
Both are my confession, both are my belief,\\
And I pray the prayer of the dying thief.
\finishverse

\medskip


\startverse{4}
Plagas, sicut Thomas, non
intúeor:\\
Deum tamen meum te confíteor:\\
Fac me tibi semper magis
crédere,\\
In te spem habére, te dilígere.
\divverse
I am not like Thomas, wounds I cannot see,\\
But can plainly call Thee Lord and God as he;\\
This faith each day deeper be my holding of,\\
Daily make me harder hope and dearer love.
\finishverse

\medskip

\startverse{5}
O memoriále mortis Dómini,\\
Panis vivus vitam præstans
hómini,\\
Præsta meæ menti de te vívere,\\
Et te illi semper dulce sápere.
\divverse
O Thou, our reminder of Christ crucified,\\
Living Bread, the life of us for whom He died,\\
Lend this life to me then; feed and feast my
mind,\\
There be Thou the sweetness man was meant to
find.
\finishverse

\medskip

\startverse{6}
Pie pellicáne Jesu Dómine,\\
Me immúndum munda tuo
sánguine,\\
Cujus una stilla salvum fácere\\
Totum mundum quit ab omni
scélere.
\divverse
Like what tender tales tell, of the Pelican\\
Bathe me, Jesus Lord, in what Thy bosom
ran\\
Blood that but one drop of, has the pow’r to
win\\
All the world forgiveness, of its world of sin.
\finishverse

\medskip

\startverse{7}
Jesu, quem velátum nunc
aspício,\\
Oro fiat illud quod tam sítio:\\
Ut te reveláta cernens fácie,\\
Visu sim beátus tuæ glóriæ.\\
Amen.
\divverse
Jesu, whom I look at shrouded here below,\\
I beseech Thee send me what I long for so,\\
Some day to gaze on Thee face to face in
light\\
And be blest forever with Thy glory’s sight.\\
Amen.
\finishverse
