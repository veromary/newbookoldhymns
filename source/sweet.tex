Hid in Thy earthly home,\\
Lo! round Thy lowly shrine,\\
\phantom{tab} With suppliant hearts we come.\\
Jesus, to Thee our voice we raise,\\
In songs of love and heartfelt praise.\\
\phantom{tab} Sweet Sacrament divine.

~

\hskip-3pt 2. Sweet Sacrament of peace,\\
\phantom{tab} Dear home of every heart,\\
Where restless yearnings cease,\\
\phantom{tab} And sorrows all depart.\\
There in Thine ear all trustfully\\
We tell our tale of misery,\\
\phantom{tab} Sweet Sacrament of peace.

\hskip-3pt 3. Sweet Sacrament of rest,\\
\phantom{tab} Ark from the ocean's roar,\\
Within Thy shelter blest\\
\phantom{tab} Soon may we reach the shore.\\
Save us, for still the tempest raves;\\
Save, lest we sink beneath the waves,\\
\phantom{tab} Sweet Sacrament of rest.

~

\hskip-3pt 4. Sweet Sacrament divine,\\
\phantom{tab} Earth's light and jubilee,\\
In Thy far depths doth shine\\
\phantom{tab} Thy Godhead's majesty.\\
Sweet light, so shine on us, we pray,\\
That earthly joys may fade away,\\
\phantom{tab} Sweet Sacrament divine.
