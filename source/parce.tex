\bigskip

\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{2.} 
Nostris malis offéndimus\\
Tuam Deus cleméntiam\\
Effúnde nobis désuper\\
Remíssor indulgéntiam.
\end{minipage}%
\hskip12pt
\begin{minipage}{0.50\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{2.} 
Our sins have offended Thy
divine Mercy yet pour out on us
from heaven the grace of pardon.
\end{minipage}
\bigskip

\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{3.} 
Dans tempus acceptábile,\\
Da lacrimárum rívulis\\
Laváre cordis víctimam,\\
Quam læta adúrat cáritas.
\end{minipage}%
\hskip12pt
\begin{minipage}{0.50\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{3.} 
Having given us this acceptible
time grant that we may purify
our hearts by abundant sorrow,
willingly consumed by the fires
of charity.
\end{minipage}
\bigskip

\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{4.} 
Audi, benígne Cónditor,\\
Nostras preces cum flétibus\\
In hoc sacro jejúnio\\
Fusas quadragenário.
\end{minipage}%
\hskip12pt
\begin{minipage}{0.50\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{4.} 
Hear, merciful Creator, the
prayers which we make with
tears in this holy fast of forty
days.
\end{minipage}
\bigskip

\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{5.} 
Scrutátor alme córdium,\\
Infírma tu scis vírium;\\
Ad te revérsis éxhibe\\
Remissiónis grátiam.
\end{minipage}%
\hskip12pt
\begin{minipage}{0.50\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{5.} 
Reader of hearts, Thou knowest
how weak is our strength, show
mercy to us who turn to Thee.
\end{minipage}
\bigskip
