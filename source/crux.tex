\startverse{2}
De paréntis protoplásti\\
Fraude Factor cóndolens,\\
Quando pomi noxiális\\
In necem morsu ruit:\\
Ipse lignum tunc notávit,\\
Damna ligni\lyrlink{}ut solveret. Dulce.
\divverse
He, our Maker, deeply grieving\\
That the first-made Adam fell,\\
When he ate the fruit forbidden\\
Whose reward was death and hell,\\
Marked e’en then this Tree the ruin\\
Of the first tree to dispel.
\finishverse

\startverse{3}
Hoc opus nostræ salútis\\
Ordo depopóscerat:\\
Multifórmis proditóris\\
Ars ut artem fálleret:\\
Et medélam ferret inde,\\
Hostis unde lǽserat. Crux.
\divverse
Thus the work for our salvation\\
He ordainéd to be done;\\
To the traitor’s art opposing\\
Art yet deeper than his own;\\
Thence the remedy procuring\\
Whence the fatal wound begun.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{4}
Quando venit ergo sacri\\
Plenitúdo témporis,\\
Missus est ab arce Patris\\
Natus, orbis Cónditor,\\
Atque ventre virgináli\\
Carne\lyrlink{}amíctus pródiit.\\
Dulce.
\divverse
Therefore, when at a length the
fulness\\
Of the appointed time was come,\\
He was sent, the world’s Creator,\\
From the Father’s heavenly home,\\
And was found in human fashion,\\
Offspring of the Virgin’s Womb.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{5}
Vagit infans inter arcta\\
Cónditus præsépia:\\
Membra pannis involúta\\
Virgo Mater álligat:\\
Et Dei manus pedésque\\
Stricta cingit fáscia. Crux.
\divverse
Lo! He lies, an infant weeping,\\
Where the narrow manger stands,\\
While the Mother-Maid His members\\
Wraps in mean and lowly bands,\\
And the swaddling-clothes is winding\\
Round His helpless feet and hands.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{6}
Lustra sex qui jam perégit,\\
Tempus implens córporis,\\
Sponte líbera Redémptor\\
Passióni déditus,\\
Agnus in Crucis levátur\\
Immolándus stípite. Dulce.
\divverse
Thirty years among us dwelling\\
His appointed time fulfilled,\\
Born for this, He meets His Passion\\
For that this He freely willed:\\
On the Cross the Lamb is lifted,\\
Where His life-blood shall be spilled.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{7}
Felle potus ecce languet:\\
Spina, clavi, láncea,\\
Mite corpus perforárunt,\\
Unda manat et cruor:\\
Terra, pontus, astra, mundus,\\
Quo lavántur flúmine! Crux.
\divverse
He endured the nails, the spitting,\\
Vinegar, and spear, and reed;\\
From that holy Body broken\\
Blood and water, forth proceed:\\
Earth and stars and sky and ocean\\
By that flood from stain are freed.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{8}
Flecte ramos, arbor alta,\\
Tensa laxa víscera,\\
Et rigor lentéscat ille,\\
Quem dedit natívitas:\\
Et supérni membra Regis\\
Tende miti stípite. Dulce.
\divverse
Bend thy boughs, O Tree of Glory!\\
Thy too rigid sinews bend;\\
And awhile the stubborn hardness,\\
Which thy birth bestow’d, suspend;\\
And King of heavenly beauty\\
On thy bosom gently tend.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{9}
Sola digna tu fuísti\\
Ferre mundi víctimam:\\
Atque portum præparáre\\
Arca mundo náufrago:\\
Quam sacer cruor perúnxit,\\
Fusus Agni córpore. Crux.
\divverse
Thou alone wast counted worthy\\
This world’s ransom to uphold,\\
For a shipwrecked race preparing\\
Harbour, like the Ark of old;\\
With the sacred Blood anointed\\
From the smitten Lamb that rolled.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{10}
Sempitérna sit beátæ\\
Trinitáti glória:\\
Æqua Patri Filióque;\\
Par decus Paráclito:\\
Uníus Triníque nomen\\
Laudet univérsitas. Amen.\\
Dulce.
\divverse
To the Trinity be glory\\
Everlasting as is meet;\\
Equal to the Father, equal\\
To the Son and Paraclete;\\
Trinal Unity, whose praises\\
All created things repeat.\\
Amen.
\finishverse
