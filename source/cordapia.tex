
\startverse{1}
Absit nobis gloriári,\\
Nisi\lyrlink{}in Cruce salutári\\
Francísci vestígio.
\divverse
Far be it from us to glory, save in
the healing Cross, in the footsteps
of Francis.
\finishverse

\vskip3pt
\startverse{2}
Nam in monte vir devótus,\\
Vigil, nudus, ardens totus\\
Crebra dat suspíria.
\divverse
For the devoted man on the
mountain, keeping watch, naked,
wholly burning, frequently sighs.
\finishverse

\vskip3pt
\startverse{3}
Solus ergo cláusus orans,\\
Super gestis Crucis plorans,\\
Mœróre confícitur.
\divverse
Therefore, alone, praying with
tears meditating on the history
of the Cross, he is transfixed with
sorrow.
\finishverse

\startverse{4}
Cernit servus Redemptórem,\\
Sæculórum Imperatórem,\\
Passum impassíbilem.
\divverse
The servant sees his Redeemer,
the commander of ages, the
unassailable assailed.
\finishverse

\startverse{5}
Cor Francísci transformátur,\\
Corpus vero mox ornátur\\
Mirándis stigmátibus.
\divverse
Francis’ heart is transformed; soon
indeed his body is adorned with the
marvellous Stigmata.
\finishverse

\startverse{6}
Crucifíxi ergo Christi\\
Mors et vita fuit isti\\
Jugis meditátio.
\divverse
Therefore the life and death of
Christ crucified was his constant
meditation.
\finishverse

\startverse{7}
Cujus cordis vim fevóris\\
Osténdit per membra foris\\
Stígmatum impréssio.
\divverse
The impression of the Stigmata,
shows the force of his heart’s
fervour outwardly through his
limbs.
\finishverse

\startverse{8}
Crucifíxe, singulári\\
Modo Cruci conformári\\
Mente fac et hábitu.
\divverse
O Crucifed One, grant that we
may be conformed in our mind
and actions to the pattern of the
Cross.
\finishverse

\startverse{9}
Fac ut nos in regno lucis\\
Perfruámur fructu Crucis,\\
Quo lætémur cǽlitus.
\divverse
Make us benefit from the fruit of
the Cross in the kingdom of light
where we may rejoice with the
heavenly one.
\finishverse

\startverse{10}
Ad quem venit Rex e cælo,\\
Affixúsque Crucis telo\\
Aspéctu pacífico.
\divverse
To whom the King of Heaven
came, fastened to the cross with
serene countenance.
\finishverse

\startverse{11}
Collaudétur Crucifíxus,\\
Francíscus prorsus inníxus\\
Super mundi foédera.
\divverse
Let the Crucified One be praised
with Francis who is resting above
the plottings of the world.
\finishverse
