\startverse{2}
Cujus ánimam geméntem,\\
Contristátem et doléntem\\
Pertransívit gládius.
\divverse
Through her heart, His sorrow\\
\phantom{big tab}sharing,\\
All His bitter anguish bearing,\\
Now at length the sword had pass’d.
\finishverse

\startverse{3}
O quam tristis et afflícta\\
Fuit illa benedícta\\
Mater Unigéniti!
\divverse
Oh, how sad and sore distresséd\\
Was that mother highly blesséd\\
Of the sole-begotten One!
\finishverse


\startverse{4}
Quæ mærébat et dolébat,\\
Pia Mater, dum vidébat\\
Nati pœnas ínclyti.
\divverse
Christ above in torment hangs;\\
She beneath beholds the pangs\\
Of her dying glorious Son.
\finishverse


\startverse{5}
Quis est homo qui non fleret,\\
Matrem Christi si vidéret\\
In tanto supplício?
\divverse
Is there one who would not weep,\\
Whelm’d in miseries so deep,\\
Christ’s dear Mother to behold?
\finishverse


\startverse{6}
Quis non posset contristári,\\
Christi Matrem contemplári\\
Doléntem cum Fílio?
\divverse
Can the human heart refrain\\
From partaking in her pain\\
In that Mother’s pain untold?
\finishverse


\startverse{7}
Pro peccátis suæ gentis,\\
Vidit Jesum in torméntis,\\
Et flagéllus súbditum.
\divverse
Bruis’d, derided, curs’d, defil’d,\\
She beheld her tender Child,\\
All with bloody scourges rent.
\finishverse


\startverse{8}
Vidit suum dulcem Natum\\
Moriéndo desolátum,\\
Dum emísit spíritum.
\divverse
For the sins of His own nation,\\
Saw Him hang in desolation,\\
Till His spirit forth He sent.
\finishverse


\startverse{9}
Eia Mater, fons amóris,\\
Me sentíre vim dolóris\\
Fac, ut tecum lúgeam.
\divverse
O thou Mother! fount of love!\\
Touch my spirit from above,\\
Make my heart with thine accord.
\finishverse


\startverse{10}
Fac ut árdeat cor meum\\
In amándo Christum Deum,\\
Ut sibi compláceam.
\divverse
Make me feel as thou hast felt:\\
Make my soul to glow and melt\\
With the love of Christ my Lord.
\finishverse


\startverse{11}
Sancta Mater, istud agas,\\
Crucifíxi fige plagas\\
Cordi meo válide.
\divverse
Holy Mother, pierce me through,\\
In my heart each wound renew\\
Of my Saviour crucified.
\finishverse


\startverse{12}
Tui Nati vulneráti,\\
Tam dignáti pro me pati,\\
Pœnas mecum dívide.
\divverse
Let me share with thee His pain,\\
Who for all my sins was slain,\\
Who for me in torments died.
\finishverse


\startverse{13}
Fac me tecum pie flere,\\
Crucifíxo condolére,\\
Donec ego víxero.
\divverse
Let me mingle tears with thee.\\
Mourning him who mourned for me,\\
All the days that I may live.
\finishverse


\startverse{14}
Juxta Crucem tecum stare,\\
Et me tibi sociáre\\
In planctu desídero.
\divverse
By the Cross with thee to stay,\\
There with thee to weep and pray,\\
All the days that I may live.
\finishverse


\startverse{15}
Virgo vírginem præclára,\\
Mihi jam non sis amára:\\
Fac me tecum plángere.
\divverse
Virgin of all virgins blest,\\
Listen to my fond request:\\
Let me share thy grief divine.
\finishverse


\startverse{16}
Fac ut portem Christi mortem\\
Passiónis fac consórtem,\\
Et plagas recólere.
\divverse
Let me, to my latest breath,\\
In my body bear the death\\
Of that dying Son of thine.
\finishverse


\startverse{17}
Fac me plagis vulnerári,\\
Fac me Cruce inebriári,\\
Et cruóre Fílii.
\divverse
Wounded with His every wound,\\
Steep my soul till it has swoon’d\\
In His very Blood away.
\finishverse


\startverse{18}
Flammis ne urar succénsus,\\
Per te, Virgo, sim defénsus\\
In die judícii.
\divverse
Be to me, O Virgin, nigh\\
Lest in flames I burn and die,\\
In His awful Judgement day.
\finishverse


\startverse{19}
Christe, cum sit hinc exíre,\\
Da per Matrem me veníre\\
Ad palmam victóriæ.
\divverse
Christ, when Thou shalt call me\\
\phantom{big tab} hence,\\
Be Thy Mother my defence,\\
Be Thy cross my victory.
\finishverse


\startverse{20}
Quando corpus moriétur,\\
Fac ut ánimæ donétur\\
Paradísi glória. Amen.
\divverse
While my body here decays,\\
May my soul Thy goodness praise,\\
Safe in Paradise with Thee. Amen.
\finishverse

\bigskip

Translation Fr.~E.~Caswall 1814--78}
