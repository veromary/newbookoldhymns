\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{2.} Veni, veni, Adonái,\\
Qui pópulo in Sínai\\
Legem dedísti vértice\\
In majestáte glóriæ.
\end{minipage}%
\begin{minipage}{0.55\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{2.} O come, O come Thou Lord of might\\
Who to Thy tribes on Sinai’s height\\
In ancient times didst give the law\\
In cloud and majesty and awe.
\end{minipage}

\bigskip

\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{3.} Veni, O Jesse Vírgula;\\
Ex hostis tuos úngula,\\
De specu tuos tártari\\
Educ, et antro bárathri.
\end{minipage}%
\begin{minipage}{0.55\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{3.} O come Thou Rod of Jesse free\\
Thine own from Satan’s tyranny;\\
From depths of hell Thy people save\\
and give them vict’ry o’er the grave.
\end{minipage}

\bigskip

\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{4.} Veni, Clavis Davídica,\\
Regna reclúde cǽlica,\\
Fac iter tutum supérnum,\\
Et claude vias ínferum.
\end{minipage}%
\begin{minipage}{0.55\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{4.} O come Thou Key of David, come\\
And open wide our heavnely home;\\
Make safe the way that leads on high\\
And close the path to misery.
\end{minipage}


\begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
Veni, veni Emmanuel\\
et ransom captivus Israel\\
Rejoice.
\end{minipage}\begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
O come, o come Emmanuel\\
And ransom captive Israel\\
That dwells in lonely exile here\\
Until the Son of God appear.
\end{minipage}
