
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.5\textwidth}
\lettrine{G}{lory} be to Jesus\\
Who in bitter pains\\
Pour’d for me the life-blood\\
From His sacred veins.


\vskip3pt\hskip-3pt 2. Grace and life eternal\\
In that blood I find:\\
Blest be His compassion,\\
Infinitely kind.


\vskip3pt\hskip-3pt 3. Blest through endless ages\\
Be the precious stream,\\
Which from endless torment\\
Doth the world redeem.


\vskip3pt\hskip-3pt 4. There the fainting spirit\\
Drinks of life her fill;\\
There as in a fountain\\
Laves herself at will.
\end{minipage}
\hskip3pt
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.5\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt 5. Abel’s blood for vengeance\\
Pleaded to the skies;\\
But the blood of Jesus\\
For our pardon cries.


\vskip3pt\hskip-3pt 6. Oft as it is sprinkled\\
On our guilty hearts,\\
Satan in confusion\\
Terror–struck departs.


\vskip3pt\hskip-3pt 7. Oft as earth exalting\\
Wafts its praise on high,\\
Hell with horror trembles;\\
Heav’n is filled with joy.

\vskip3pt\hskip-3pt 8. Lift ye, then, your voices;\\
Swell the mighty flood;\\
Louder still and louder,\\
Praise the Precious Blood.
\end{minipage}

\medskip

\mysource{Translated by Fr. E. Caswall, 1814--78}
