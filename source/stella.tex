\vskip-5mm%
\hskip-5mm%
\startverse{2}
Principes et magnates ex stirpe regia,\\
Sæculi potestates obtenta venia\\
Peccaminum proclamant tundentes\\
\phantom{tab} pectora,\\
Poplite flexo clamant hic: Ave Maria.
\divverse
Leaders and magnates of royal
lineagae, great men of the world who
possess grace proclaim their sins and
beat their breasts and cry on bended
knee: Hail Mary.
\finishverse

\hskip-15mm%
\startverse{3}
Prælati et barones, comites incliti,\\
Religiosi omnes atque presbyteri,\\
Milites, mercatores, cives, marinari,\\
Burgenses, piscatores præmiantur ibi.
\divverse
Prelates and barons with their
celebrated retinues, all monks and
priests, soldiers, merchants, citizens,
sailors, townspeople and fishermen
sing praises here.
\finishverse

\hskip-10mm%
\startverse{4}
Rustici aratores, nec non notarii,\\
Advocati, scultores, cuncti ligni fabri,\\
Sartores et sutores, nec non lanifici\\
Artifices et omnes gratulantur ibi.
\divverse
Farmers, ploughmen and also
scribes, advocates, sculptors and all
carpenters, tailors and cobblers and all
weavers; all artisans rejoice here.
\finishverse

\startverse{5}
Reginæ, comitissæ, illustres dominæ,\\
Potentes et ancillæ, juvenes parvulæ,\\
Virgines et antiquæ pariter viduæ\\
Conscendunt et hunc montem et\\
\phantom{tab} religiosæ.
\divverse
Queens, noblewomen, illustrious ladies
of power and their handmaidens,
young girls, virgins, old women and
widows, climb the mountain, as do
nuns.
\finishverse

\startverse{6}
Cœtus hi aggregantur, hic ut\\
\phantom{tab} exhibeant\\
Vota, regratiantur, ut ipsa et reddant\\
Aulam istam ditantes, hoc cuncti\\
\phantom{tab} videant,\\
Jocalibus ornantes, soluti redeant.
\divverse
The assembly is gathered here to
declare to you a vow, and to give
thanks; to fulfil this vow in this
glorious place, so all may see you
return in joy, and all may share your
salvation.
\finishverse

\startverse{7}
Cuncti ergo precantes, sexus\\
\phantom{tab} utriusque,\\
Mentes nostras mundantes oremus\\
\phantom{tab} devote\\
Virginem gloriosam, Matrem\\
\phantom{tab} clementiæ,\\
In cœlis gratiosam sentiamus vere.
\divverse
So shall we all pray, both men
and women, and in true humility
acknowledge our sins to the glorious
Virgin, Mother of clemency, so that
we may be favoured with Heaven.
\finishverse
