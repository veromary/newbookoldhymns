Principes et magnates ex stirpe regia,\\

Peccaminum proclamant tundentes

\divverse
Leaders and magnates of royal
lineagae, great men of the world who
possess grace proclaim their sins and
beat their breasts and cry on bended
knee: Hail Mary.
