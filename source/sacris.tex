\startverse{2}

Qua Christus créditur agnum et\\
\phantom{tab} ázyma\\

\divverse
Now we remember the supper of
that last evening, in which we
know that Christ gave the paschal
lamb and the unleavened bread to
the brethren, according to the law
of the ancient fathers.
\finishverse

\divverse
After the symbol of the lamb,
supper being over, we believe that
the Body of the Lord was given to
the disciples by His own hands,
whole to all and whole to each
one.
\finishverse

\startverse{4}

\phantom{tab} vásculum,\\

\divverse
To the weak He gave His
strengthening Body, to the sad
the cup of His Blood, saying:
Receive what I give you, drink ye
all of this.
\finishverse

\medskip


\startverse{5}

Cujus offícium commítti vóluit\\

cóngruit.\\
Ut sumant, et dent céteris.
\divverse
So He founded this sacrifice,
which He committed to priests
alone, that they should partake
and give to the others.
\finishverse

\medskip

\startverse{6}

\phantom{tab} hóminum:\\

\phantom{tab} Dóminum\\

\divverse
The bread of angels becomes bread
of men; the heavenly food makes
an end of symbols. O wonderful
thing, a poor and lowly servant
eats the Body of the Lord.
\finishverse

\medskip

\startverse{7}

Per tuas sémitas duc nos quo\\
\phantom{tab} téndimus,\\

\divverse
We pray Thee, Godhead three and
one, come to us as we worship
Thee; lead us by Thy path to the
goal for which we hope, to the
light in which Thou dwellest.
Amen.
\finishverse
