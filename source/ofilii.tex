\bigskip

\startverse{2}
Et mane prima sabbati,\\
Ad ostium monumenti\\
Accesserunt discipuli,\\
\phantom{tab}alleluia.
\divverse
On Sunday morn by break of day,\\
His dear disciples haste away\\
Unto the tomb wherein He lay,\\
\phantom{tab}alleluia.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{3}
Et Maria Magdalene,\\
Et Jacobi et Salome,\\
Venerunt corpus ungere,\\
\phantom{tab}alleluia.
\divverse
Nor Magdalen, nor Salome,\\
Nor James’ mother now delay\\
To embalm the precious corpse\\
\phantom{tab}straightway, alleluia.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{4}
In albis sedens Angelus\\
Praedixit mulieribus:\\
In Galilaea est Dominus,\\
\phantom{tab}alleluia.
\divverse
An Angel clothed in white they see,\\
When thither come, and thus spake\\
he,\\
“The Lord is gone to Galilee.”\\
\phantom{tab}alleluia.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{5}
Et Joannes Apostolus\\
Cucurrit Petro citius,\\
Monumento venit prius,\\
\phantom{tab}alleluia.
\divverse
The dear beloved apostle, John\\
Much swifter than St. Peter run,\\
And first arrived at the tomb,\\
\phantom{tab}alleluia.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{6}
Discipulis astantibus,\\
In medio stetit Christus,\\
Dicens : Pax vobis\\
\phantom{tab}omnibus, alleluia
\divverse
That night th’apostles met in fear;\\
Amidst them came their Lord most\\
\phantom{tab}dear,\\
And said, “My peace be on all here,”\\
\phantom{tab}alleluia.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{7}
Ut intellexit Didymus\\
Quia surrexerat Jesus,\\
Remansit fere dubius, alleluia.
\divverse
When Thomas first the tidings heard,\\
how they had seen the risen Lord,\\
he doubted the disciples’ word,\\
alleluia.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{8}
Vide Thoma, vide latus,\\
Vide pedes, vide manus,\\
Noli esse incredulus, alleluia.
\divverse
“My piercéd side, O Thomas, see;\\
My hands, My feet, I show to thee;\\
not faithless, but believing be,”\\
\phantom{tab}alleluia.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{9}
Quando Thomas Christi\\
\phantom{tab}latus,\\
Pedes vidit atque manus,\\
Dixit : Tu es Deus meus,\\
\phantom{tab}alleluia.
\divverse
No longer Thomas then denied,\\
he saw the feet, the hands, the side;\\
“Thou art my Lord and God,” he\\
\phantom{tab}cried, alleluia.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{10}
Beati qui non viderunt,\\
Et firmiter crediderunt,\\
Vitam aeternam habebunt,\\
\phantom{tab}alleluia.
\divverse
How blest are they who have not\\
\phantom{tab}seen,\\
And yet whose faith has constant been,\\
For they eternal life shall win, alleluia.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{11}
In hoc festo sanctissimo\\
Sit laus et jubilatio,\\
\textsc{Benedicamus Domino,}\\
alleluia.
\divverse
On this most solemn feast let’s raise\\
Our hearts to God in hymns of praise,\\
And let us bless the Lord always,\\
alleluia.
\finishverse

\bigskip

\startverse{12}
De quibus nos humillimas\\
Devotas atque debitas\\
\textsc{Deo} dicamus \textsc{Gratias,}\\
alleluia.
\divverse
Our grateful thanks to God let’s give\\
In humble manner, while we live,\\
For all the favours we receive, alleluia.
\finishverse
