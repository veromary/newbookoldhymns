\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{2.} 
Deus homo factus est,\\
Natura mirante,\\
Mundus renovatus est\\
A Christo regnante.
\end{minipage}%
\begin{minipage}{0.55\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{2.} God is made man,\\
While nature wonders\\
The world is renewed\\
By Christ the King.
\end{minipage}

\smallskip

\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{3.} 
Ezechielis porta\\
Clausa per transitur,\\
Unde lux est orta\\
Salus invenitur.
\end{minipage}%
\begin{minipage}{0.55\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{3.}
The closed gate of Ezekiel\\
Has been passed through;\\
Whence the light has risen,\\
Salvation is found.
\end{minipage}

\smallskip

\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt{4.} 
Ergo nostra contio\\
Psallat jam in lustro,\\
Benedicat Domino,\\
Salus Regi nostro.
\end{minipage}%
\begin{minipage}{0.55\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{4.} 
Therefore let our assembly\\
Sing praises at this time of purification\\
Let us bless the Lord:\\
Greetings to our King.
\end{minipage}
