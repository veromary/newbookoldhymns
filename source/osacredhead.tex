\lettrine{O}{ Sacred Head!} sore wounded,\\
\phantom{tab} With grief and shame \\
	\phantom{big tab} weighed down,\\
O Kingly Head! surrounded\\
\phantom{tab} With thorns, Thy only crown;\\
Death's pallor now comes o'er Thee,\\
\phantom{tab} The glow of life decays,\\
Yet hosts of heaven adore Thee\\
\phantom{tab} And tremble as they gaze.

	\ 

	\hskip-3pt{2.} 
What language shall I borrow\\
\phantom{tab} To thank Thee, dearest Friend,\\
For this Thy dying sorrow,\\
\phantom{tab} Thy pity without end?\\
O agony and dying!\\
\phantom{tab} O love to sinners free!\\
Jesus, all grace supplying,\\
\phantom{tab} Turn Thou Thy face on me.
\end{minipage}
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.5\textwidth}
3. In this thy bitter passion\\
\phantom{tab} Sweet Jesus, think of me.\\
With thy most sweet compassion,\\
\phantom{tab} Unworthy though I be:\\
Beneath thy cross abiding\\
\phantom{tab} Forever would I rest,\\
In thy dear love confiding,\\
\phantom{tab} And with thy presence blest.

	\ 

4. Be thou my consolation,\\
\phantom{tab} My shield, when I must die;\\
Remind me of thy passion\\
\phantom{tab} When my last hour draws nigh.\\
Mine eyes shall then behold thee;\\
\phantom{tab} Upon thy cross shall dwell,\\
My heart by faith enfold thee;\\
\phantom{tab} Who dieth thus, dies well.
\end{minipage}
