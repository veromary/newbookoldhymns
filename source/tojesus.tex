
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.5\textwidth}
\lettrine{T}{o Jesus’ Heart} all burning\\
With fervent love for men,\\
My yet untold desire\\
God gives me for Thy sake.

\vskip3pt\hskip-3pt Ant. While ages course along,\\
Blest be with loudest song\\
The Sacred Heart of Jesus\\
By every heart and tongue.
\end{minipage}
\hskip3pt
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.5\textwidth}
\hskip-3pt 2. O Heart, for me on fire\\
With love no man can speak,\\
My heart with fondest yearning\\
Shall raise its joyful strain.


\vskip3pt\hskip-3pt 3. When life away is flying,\\
And earth’s false glare is done;\\
Still, Sacred Heart, in dying\\
I’ll say I’m all Thy own.
\end{minipage}

\smallskip

Translated by A.~J.~Christie, S.J.,1817--91}
