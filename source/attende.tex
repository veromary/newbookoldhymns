\end{minipage}%
\hskip12pt
\begin{minipage}{0.49\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{3.} 
We, Thy eternal majesty
entreating,\\
with Thy blessed ears hear our
sighing:\\
graciously grant pardon to our sins.
\end{minipage}

\bigskip



\noindent\begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}

admíssa:\\
contríto corde pándimus
occúlta:\\

ignóscat.
\end{minipage}%
\hskip12pt
\begin{minipage}{0.49\textwidth}
\itshape
\hskip-3pt{4.} 
Humbly confess we, who have
sinned against Thee,\\
with contrite hearts we reveal things
hidden;\\
O Redeemer, may Thy pity grant
forgiveness.
\end{minipage}
