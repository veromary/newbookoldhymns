For some reason I've been running a weekly chant zoom, running through a piece then playing an online quiz to consolidate vocabulary and the odd bit of neume reading concepts.

It has been 11.30am on Fridays for a long time, but this year there are plans afoot for a play in honour of St Wenceslas for which the rehearsals will take place on Fridays. 

First continue 11.30am Fridays for February:

* Feb 6 - Attende Domine
* Feb 13 - Parce Domine
* Feb 20 - Ave Regina Caelorum
* Feb 27 - Ave Verum Corpus

Then switch to 1pm Mondays in March:

* Mar 2 - Gloria Laus
* Mar 9 - Vexilla Regis
* Mar 16 - Ubi Caritas
* Mar 23 - Stabat Mater
* Mar 30 - Pange Lingua

How's that?

There are 10 pieces in the [Lessons](/lessons) so we won't quite get up to Crux Fidelis, but we could go crazy and include both Pange Linguas on the last session. Or just acknowledge there are two Pange Linguas. Maybe have a vote whether Aquinas or Fortunatus.
